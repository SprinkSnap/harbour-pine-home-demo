## Project overview

Harbour & Pine Home is a modern Canadian home and lifestyle storefront concept. It shows how Che Xu Studio designs calm, product-focused commerce experiences with clear discovery, accessible interactions and conversion systems based on trust rather than pressure.

## Intended merchant audience

- Home and lifestyle brands
- Specialty retailers preparing a custom storefront
- Makers and small-batch merchants needing clearer product storytelling
- Teams replacing a generic theme with a faster, more intentional shopping journey

## Fictional business challenge

Many home brands struggle with storefronts that look interchangeable, bury product details, and rely on urgency tactics. This concept explores a better path: editorial clarity, practical information architecture and friction-aware shopping flows that help customers decide with confidence.

## Customer journey

1. Understand the brand promise in the first viewport
2. Browse by collection or room
3. Filter and search without losing context
4. Evaluate variants, materials, dimensions and care
5. Save items or add them to a demo cart
6. Complete a guest checkout demonstration
7. If inspired, request a real store plan from Che Xu Studio

## Product architecture

Typed catalogue modules define products, collections, variants, related items and SEO fields as a single source of truth. The demo includes 22 fictional products across Living, Kitchen & Dining, Textiles, Storage, Workspace and Gifts.

## Search and filtering

The shop experience supports search, collection/category/colour/price/availability filters, sorting, active-filter summaries, empty states and shareable URLs.

## Product-page strategy

Each product page emphasizes:

- breadcrumbs
- image gallery with keyboard-safe zoom
- clear price and selected variant
- materials, dimensions and care
- sample shipping/returns architecture
- related products
- Che Xu Studio portfolio CTA

No fake ratings, scarcity or crossed-out prices are used.

## Cart and checkout flow

The demo cart persists locally and clearly states that no real order will be created. Checkout walks through contact, delivery method, shipping details and review using non-transmitted sample values, then invites the visitor to build a real store with Che Xu Studio.

## Mobile design

Mobile-first layout, sticky cart access, large touch targets, accessible navigation with focus restoration, and condensed product cards keep the experience usable on small screens.

## Product SEO

The architecture prepares unique titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, breadcrumbs, sitemap, robots guidance, Open Graph tags and future verified Product/Offer schema. In demo mode the site remains `noindex, nofollow` and suppresses fabricated commerce schema.

## Image optimization

Purpose-built SVG product and brand visuals use explicit dimensions, reserved aspect ratios, lazy-loading below the fold and an eager LCP hero. The system is ready for Cloudflare Images or AVIF/WebP pipelines in a verified merchant build.

## Accessibility

The concept targets WCAG 2.2 AA patterns: skip link, semantic landmarks, keyboard-operable menus/drawers/filters/variants, visible focus, status announcements and reduced-motion support.

## Technical architecture

Astro static generation, React islands for interactivity, Tailwind design tokens, Cloudflare Workers with static assets, D1 for consented leads, Turnstile verification, rate limiting and security headers.

## Security

Lead capture uses schema validation, allowlists, request-size limits, content-type checks, origin validation, honeypot, Turnstile server verification, prepared D1 statements and personal-data redaction in logs.
